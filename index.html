Lagos

Lagos State, located in the southwestern region of Nigeria along the coast of the Bight of Benin, is the country's smallest state by land area yet its most populous and vibrant economic engine. Created on May 27, 1967, and having served as Nigeria's capital until 1991, the state—with its capital in Ikeja—operates as a major financial, cultural, and maritime hub for West Africa. Despite challenges like overcrowding and traffic, it drives a massive share of the national economy and is widely known by its motto, the "Centre of Excellence".
